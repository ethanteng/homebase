Storage for Uncloud

Add as much storage as you need.

Uncloud keeps your family’s files on a computer you already own, or on a drive plugged into it. There are no storage tiers: pick a drive with enough room for your family.

Drives by size and type.

A portable SSD is small, fast and silent. A portable hard drive costs less, up to 5 TB. A desktop drive holds the most for the money. Add up what your family keeps in Dropbox, Google Drive and iCloud today, then leave room to grow.

Plug it in and point Uncloud at it.

  1. 1

    Connect the drive to your host

    Plug it into the Mac, PC or Linux computer that runs Uncloud, and leave it connected.

  2. 2

    Choose a folder on it

    During setup, pick a folder on the drive and click Use this folder. Everyone’s files go inside it and share its free space.

  3. 3

    Watch the space left

    Every page in Uncloud shows how much room is left, and turns amber, then red, as it runs low. Add a bigger drive when you need one.
