Your private cloud, in 3 steps.

Set up on your Mac in 5 minutes or less.

  1. 1

    Pick your computer

    Use a Mac you already own with room for your files. Keep it on and awake so your files stay available.

  2. 2

    Make it your cloud

    Install Uncloud, create your account and choose where your files will live. Your computer or a connected drive provides the space.

  3. 3

    Bring your files home

    Copy files from Dropbox or folders on your computer. Turn on remote access to reach them from your phone, tablet or laptop.

Start with the Mac app today. Linux is also supported with manual setup. For Google Drive or iCloud, download your files to your computer first.
